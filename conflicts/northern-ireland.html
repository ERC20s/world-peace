Overview

Northern Ireland experienced decades of sectarian conflict known as "The Troubles" (late 1960s–1998). The 1998 Good Friday Agreement substantially reduced large-scale violence; ongoing community-level reconciliation, cross-community programs, and support for victims are part of continuing peace-building efforts. This page lists neutral, verifiable initiatives and organisations and sources for further reading.

Initiatives and organisations

  • Community Relations Council (CRC) — supports peace-building projects across Northern Ireland, offering grants and research into reconciliation. Community Relations Council — grants and research into reconciliation.
  • Corrymeela Community — Northern Ireland's longest-running peace and reconciliation charity, running residential and community programs to promote contact and understanding. Corrymeela Community — residential and community reconciliation programmes.
  • WAVE Trauma Centre — provides trauma counselling and support services to victims of the conflict, contributing to community recovery. WAVE Trauma Centre — trauma counselling and victim support services.
