Overview

Country / region: Northern Ireland.
Website: https://www.corrymeela.org/
Mission: Corrymeela describes itself as Ireland's oldest peace and reconciliation organisation, working to build relationships across religious, political and social divides through residential programmes, community engagement and education. Source.

Verifiable activities

  • Runs residential reconciliation programmes at its centre on the north Antrim coast, bringing together people from different community backgrounds. Source.
  • Delivers community-relations and education programmes for schools and youth groups across Northern Ireland. Source.
  • Is referenced on the Northern Ireland conflict page as the region's longest-running peace and reconciliation charity. Northern Ireland conflict page.
