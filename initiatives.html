  • WANEP (West Africa Network for Peacebuilding) — A regional peacebuilding organisation, headquartered in Accra, that supports early-warning and conflict-prevention work including in Liberia.

  • WIPNET (Women in Peacebuilding Network) — A program of the West Africa Network for Peacebuilding that organised and trained women peace activists in Liberia during and after the conflict.
    Liberia · Capacity building · Started 2002 · Run by West Africa Network for Peacebuilding (WANEP) · Liberia page · Source
  • Women of Liberia Mass Action for Peace — A grassroots movement of Christian and Muslim women, led by Leymah Gbowee, that organised nonviolent protests and sit-ins credited with helping press combatants toward the 2003 Accra talks.
    Liberia · Civil society mobilisation · Started 2003 · Run by Women of Liberia Mass Action for Peace · Liberia page · Source
  • Truth and Reconciliation Commission of Liberia — Established under the 2003 Accra Comprehensive Peace Agreement to investigate human rights violations from 1979 to 2003 and recommend reconciliation measures.
    Liberia · Transitional justice · Started 2005 · Run by Truth and Reconciliation Commission of Liberia · Liberia page · Source
  • Corrymeela Community — Northern Ireland's longest-running peace and reconciliation charity, running residential and community programs to promote contact and understanding.
