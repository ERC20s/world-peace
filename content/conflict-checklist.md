# Conflict page checklist

Use this checklist together with templates/conflict-template.html when
drafting a new conflicts/<name>.html page. Every item marked REQUIRED must be
satisfied before the page is submitted for review.

Structure (REQUIRED)
- Title and one-sentence neutral summary in the page header.
- Overview section with a short, factual background/timeline.
- Initiatives and organisations section with at least three entries.
- "What a reader can do today" section.
- Sources section.

Verifiability (REQUIRED)
- Every organisation entry links to that organisation's own site or a
  reputable third-party report — not a social media post or forum.
- Every source in the Sources section includes: a title, a URL, a
  publication date, and a type (news, report, primary document, or
  organisation site). Example line format:
  - Community Relations Council annual report — organisation site, 2023.
    https://www.community-relations.org.uk/
- Non-obvious factual claims in the Overview section are traceable to a
  source listed on the same page.
- Prefer primary sources (official statements, government or NGO reports)
  and established secondary sources (major news outlets) over blogs or
  unverified sites.
- Where public, an organisation's contact page or press contact may be
  linked so readers can verify claims directly.

Tone (REQUIRED)
- Neutral, factual language throughout; do not take sides in the conflict.
- No fundraising language and no donation links anywhere on the page.
- "What a reader can do today" lists only non-fundraising actions: reading,
  volunteering directly with an organisation, or sharing reputable sources.
  Do not phrase these as calls to donate or as advocacy campaigns.

Before opening a pull request
- Confirm the page uses templates/conflict-template.html as its starting
  point and that every REQUIRED comment marker has been replaced with real
  content (no placeholder text such as "Organisation name" remains).
- Confirm all links resolve and use https where available.
- Add the new page to index.html's conflict list.
